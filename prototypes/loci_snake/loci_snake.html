Locus Serpent Plot simulated data

Each locus drops a translucent snake from its genome position through every dataset. The snake turns green, then yellow, then red, and gets thicker at each step, as the locus becomes more significant in that dataset. Its sideways drift shows how far that dataset's lead SNP sits from the locus center. Loci shared across diseases show up as long, thick snakes.
