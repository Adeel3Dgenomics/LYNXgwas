Building the Locus Serpent Plot and running the category analysis…

Each locus drops a serpent from its genomic position through every dataset. At each crossing, color and thickness show that dataset's significance, and sideways drift shows how far its lead SNP sits from the strongest dataset's lead SNP. Loci shared across diseases appear as long, thick red serpents.
