Building the Locus Serpent Plot and running the category analysis…

Each locus drops a serpent from its genomic position through every dataset. At each crossing, color and thickness show that dataset's significance, and sideways drift shows how far its lead SNP sits from the strongest dataset's lead SNP. Loci shared across diseases appear as long, thick red serpents. Click a serpent to see each dataset's lead SNP, OR and p-value.

Download table (TSV)
One row per joint credible set: its size, PIP range, lead SNP, the datasets it is active in, and the smallest credible set any single dataset gives for the same lead SNP. Open a locus to see its result, compare single-dataset and joint PIPs, or draw it on a regional plot.

Sideways drift
Offset of each dataset's lead SNP from the lead SNP of the strongest dataset at that locus: ±150 kb is drawn as ±14 px (capped at ±300 kb). A bend means that dataset peaks somewhere else in the locus.

What drives the clustering?

Which dataset category best explains which loci are significant together, and which loci are consistently significant within each group. Click a category to reorder the plot by it; click a locus to highlight its serpent.
