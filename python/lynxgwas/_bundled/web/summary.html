Fine-mapping: how many signals per locus

SuSiE credible sets per locus. One set means one independent causal signal; zero means the locus could not be resolved with this LD reference.

Fine-mapping: how precise are the credible sets

Number of SNPs in each 95% credible set. Smaller is better: a set of 1–5 SNPs points to a short list of candidate causal variants.

Causal-SNP ranking: confidence per locus

Score of each locus's top-ranked SNP: the probability that it is the locus's causal variant, combining fine-mapping, function, and replication in other datasets.

Top causal-SNP candidates

The most confident candidates across all loci. Click a row to open the locus.

Genes (MAGMA)

Loci with at least one gene significant in the gene-based test, with the strongest gene of each.

Regulatory enrichment

Are genome-wide-significant SNPs more often in active regulatory regions of the disease tissue than other SNPs? Odds ratio > 1 means enriched.
